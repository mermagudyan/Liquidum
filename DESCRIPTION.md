# Liquidum — liquid glass interface

Turns the whole Minecraft interface into liquid glass: buttons, the hotbar and inventory slots become translucent glass that blurs and bends the world behind them, while your items and text stay perfectly sharp on top.

- Glass buttons, sliders and tabs with a soft glow that follows the mouse
- Glass hotbar with a gliding selection frame (shrinks into a Home strip in menus)
- Inventory and container slots as glossy recessed wells on one continuous surface
- Built-in Material Lab (F7): tune the glass live and save your own look
- Russian and English interface, switches automatically

Early beta — the look is still being polished, feedback and screenshots are welcome.
